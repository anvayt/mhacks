# Analysis choices fixed before correlations

Recorded October 4, 2026, before extracting roof snow or computing outcome correlations.

- Winter: December 1, 2025 through March 31, 2026. Public Earth Search `sentinel-2-l2a`, tile 17TKG (UTM 17N, covers Ann Arbor); deduplicate processing versions of the same acquisition. Search the complete footprint bounding box. Check local SCL, not only whole-tile cloud percentage.
- NOAA station: USC00200230, ANN ARBOR U OF MICH (complete SNOW/SNWD for these four months). A snowfall event is positive SNOW with either SNOW >=25 mm and SNWD >=25 mm, or an SNWD increase >=25 mm. The 25 mm screening threshold is an analyst choice close to the inch precision of the depth record, not a validated physical roof threshold. Observation dates 1–10 days after the event, truncated before the next event to avoid fresh snow resets. Reject nonblank NOAA quality flags; retain trace/missing flags in the extracted table.
- Roof selection: >=1,500 m² (user's starting threshold), inward buffer 20 m (one B11/SCL pixel); >=3 native 20 m pixel centers inside the buffer. Upsampling B11 to 10 m does not create independent detail. Keep all qualifying roofs; compare predicted costs only where the scored-home table has an entry.
- NDSI = (green - SWIR1)/(green + SWIR1), calibrated using STAC asset scale/offset; B11 bilinear to the B03 10 m grid, SCL nearest-neighbor. Snow NDSI >0.4 (legacy NASA MODIS snow algorithm, transferred here as a screening rule, not validated specifically on roofs). Retain SCL 4/5/11 only (vegetation/bare/snow), excluding water, shadow, clouds, cirrus, uncertain and invalid pixels; require nonnegative reflectance and positive denominator.
- Ground control: ring 20–80 m from the roof, excluding every city footprint buffered by 10 m. Require at least 25 clear 10 m samples and >=50% clear ring area. Require >=80% of roof samples clear and at least 3 clear native 20 m pixel centers. These coverage cutoffs and the ring are analyst quality-control choices, fixed before checking correlation.
- Per event/building: at least two paired clear scenes separated by >=2 days, with ground snow fraction >=0.5 at the first paired observation. Use trapezoidal area of (ground snow fraction - roof snow fraction), divided by the observed interval. Average event indices equally. Positive means the roof has less persistent snow than its ground control. This is a relative snow-loss proxy, not a measured melting rate or thermal leak; do not extrapolate before/after observed scenes or call a single clear scene a curve.
- Meter join: city footprint >50% inside a public benchmarking property polygon, matching P1's existing join. Drop ambiguous multiple-property matches. Aggregate eligible roof indices by roof area to one row per metered property, avoiding pseudo-replication. Primary validation requires eligible roofs cover >=50% of the property's mapped roof area; also report the unrestricted-coverage sensitivity.
- Ground truth: restrict buildings_hc.csv to method=metered; require P1's gas_r2 >=0.7, positive heat_ccf and HDD, valid floor area and >=12 valid monthly gas records. Intensity = heat_ccf / gfa_ft2 / hdd_ref, ccf/ft²/HDD60. This is a baseline-separated, meter-fitted historical heating target, not simulated output and not a direct roof meter. Validate monthly records and report years. Include raw cold-month gas/ft²/HDD60 as a secondary sensitivity (includes non-heating gas).
- Spearman rank correlation at the independent property level, two-sided 9,999-permutation p-value (add-one correction), 10,000 property-bootstrap percentile 95% CI, seed 20261004. Average tied ranks. Undefined statistics stay null. Predicted cost/sqft is secondary and cannot trigger GO.
- GO only for primary metered rho >0, p <0.05, n >=20. Otherwise NO-GO, no endpoint and no grade changes. Report coverage/selection/confounders regardless of p. Sensitivities cannot rescue a failed primary test.

## Radiometric QA correction before final outputs

The initial asset-scale/offset implementation was corrected after an independent pixel audit, without changing geometry, events, masking or GO criteria. `earthsearch:boa_offset_applied=True` legacy COGs already have the -1000 DN offset applied even though `raster:bands` still advertises -0.1. Six same-scene green/SWIR windows (December, January, February) are compared with Collection 1 by `audit_radiometry.py`. Final legacy reflectance uses scale 0.0001 and effective offset 0 for these flagged scenes. Versioned raster caches prevent reuse of the initial double-offset products. This is a data-decoding fix, not a threshold selected for a stronger association. The primary metered sample was already zero from geometry alone.
