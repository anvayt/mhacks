### Prompt given (excerpt)
> Research the MHacks event(s) held in 2022. ### Bottom line: no MHacks event was held in 2022
I checked three independent event lists, and none of them has an MHacks-branded event dated in calendar year 2022. **So there are no 2022 winners to list.** The two closest events are **MHacks 14** (Oct 15–17, 2021, online) and **MHacks 15** (Feb 17–19, 2023, online).

**A fall 2022 event was announced but did not happen.**
- An archived copy of mhacks.org's site code (Wayback capture of `/js/app.js`, 2022-08-15) says: "MHacks is a 36-hour hackathon in October run by University of Michigan students. **This fall, we will be back to fully in-person in Ann Arbor.**" It also links an MLH trust badge for the 2023 season. ([Wayback app.js 2022-08-15](https://web.archive.org/web/20220815031753/https://mhacks.org/js/app.js))
- The same file (identical digest) was still being served at the Dec 8, 2022 capture, after October had passed. ([Wayback mhacks.org 2022-12-08](https://web.archive.org/web/20221208194741/https://mhacks.org/))
- The next actual event was MHacks 15 in Feb 2023, and it was **online**, not in person. ([Devpost MHacks 15](https://mhacks-15.devpost.com/))
- UMich CSE later described MHacks 16 (Nov 2023) as "its first in-person iteration since 2019". It said the pandemic "forced the event online," and that attendance had "shrunk to under 200 participants in 2021." ([CSE, Nov 8, 2023, archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20251011132721/https://cse.engin.umich.edu/stories/cs-students-revive-mhacks-legacy))
- *Inference:* the planned October 2022 in-person MHacks was dropped or postponed. Its slot was effectively filled by the online MHacks 15 in Feb 2023. I found no public statement explaining why. The Michigan Daily and organizer social posts could not be searched, because the session's web-search budget was used up. Treat this as an open gap.

### Patterns (what a missing year tells us)
- **2020 to early 2023 was a small, online stretch of MHacks.** MHacks 14 had 164 registrants on Devpost and MHacks 15 had 368. At its 2016 peak, MHacks drew over 2,000 participants (CSE). Winners from this stretch competed against far smaller fields, and MHacks 15 was judged by the MHacks core team (see `2023.md`). *Inference:* their winning patterns carry over to an in-person, sponsor-heavy MHacks 2026 less reliably than winners from 2023–2025.
- **Small hardware builds still won even online.** Both neighboring events gave Winner badges to physical builds: SunLite lamp, F.L.U.D.D and Smart Lock at MHacks 14, and LumiGUI at MHacks 15. *Inference from badges and taglines only:* a working physical prototype stood out even when judges watched it on video.

### Takeaways for MHacks 2026
- **Don't look for 2022 winner data. There isn't any.** Put effort into the in-person relaunch era: MHacks 16 (Nov 2023), MHacks 17 / Google x MHacks (Apr 2024), MHacks 2024 and MHacks 2025. Those formats are closest to MHacks 2026.
- **Discount pandemic-era winners** (MHacks 13 Beta, 14, 15) when copying strategy. They were online, small and judged by the core team. Today's event has sponsor-judged tracks and in-person table demos.
- If you mine the 2021/2023 neighbors anyway, the most transferable signal is that **simple, working hardware** and **clear social-good framing** (flood alerts, water quality, accessibility) were rewarded. That maps to **Beyond the Code (Hardware)** and **Sustainability** today. *(Inference.)*

### What organizers said in 2022 (the only signal there is)
- **A fall 2022 event was announced on the site, but it never took place.** The 2022-08-15 build of mhacks.org's JS includes this sentence, which I confirmed myself: "MHacks is a 36-hour hackathon in October run by University of Michigan students. This fall, we will be back to fully in-person in Ann Arbor." The Oct 2021 and Apr 2022 builds don't have that sentence. ([Wayback app.js 2022-08-15](https://web.archive.org/web/20220815031753/https://mhacks.org/js/app.js))
- **The 2022 site had nothing about judging.** In all three builds (Oct 2021, Apr 2022, Aug 2022), the only text that mentions judging is an FAQ line: "If you're contemplating judging, volunteering, or mentoring, shoot us an email at hackathon@umich.edu." The builds have no criteria, no judging format and no list of judges. (Same Wayback source.)
- **Looking back, organizers skip 2022 entirely.** In the UMich CSE story on the 2023 relaunch, the history goes straight from "under 200 participants in 2021" to the Nov 2023 event, called "its first in-person iteration since 2019". The relaunch was led by co-directors Spencer Goodwin and Adviti Mishra. They came out of the student organization V1, found leftover MHacks gear in a storage unit, and decided to "bring MHacks back". ([CSE, Nov 8 2023, archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20251011132721/https://cse.engin.umich.edu/stories/cs-students-revive-mhacks-legacy))
- *Inference:* the October 2022 in-person plan was dropped, and the 2022–23 school year's event became MHacks 15, held online in Feb 2023. I found no statement giving the reason.

### Takeaways for MHacks 2026
- **2022 has no judging data, so don't go looking for it.** If a source labels something "MHacks 2022 winners", it is almost certainly MHacks 14 (Oct 2021) or MHacks 15 (Feb 2023) under the wrong year. Check the dates on Devpost before you trust it.
- **For judging signal, rely on the relaunch era (Nov 2023 onward).** The CSE story says a new team from V1 rebuilt MHacks from scratch in 2023. *Inference:* the judging culture, sponsor mix and tracks of MHacks 2026 descend from MHacks 16, 2024 and 2025. They don't descend from the pre-2022 organization, so give pre-2023 judging evidence little weight.
- **The 2022 gap is also why MHacks 15's judging differs from today's.** MHacks 15 was small, online and judged by the core team (see `2023.md`). MHacks 2026 runs in person and has sponsor tracks. Expect sponsor-specific judging and live table demos, the format the relaunch-era files document. *(Inference.)*
