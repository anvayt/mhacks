### Patterns: why these projects won

1. **Immersive XR demos took the top prize at both 2024 events.** V²/R (VR) won MHacks 2024's Grand Prize, and Cosmocook (HoloLens AR) won 1st at Google x MHacks. AstroChat VR also placed. *Inference:* a headset demo is memorable and easy to understand in a short judging slot. V²/R won with no LLM at all, which suggests novelty and demo quality count for more than "uses AI".
2. **A specific user with a quantified problem.** NurseNotes (41% of a nurse's shift is paperwork), FocusFlow (ADHD statistics), FarmX (Michigan farmers), Easy ABLLS-R (autism clinicians), SignVerse/Msign (Deaf community), WiLi Watch (limited mobility), Visa Friend (immigrants). Six of the eight MHacks 2024 main and track winners name a specific group of people they help.
3. **Local and personal hooks.** The U-M EECS 215 lab (V²/R), Michigan agriculture (FarmX), a teammate's late uncle (SignVerse), the team's NASA AR project (Cosmocook), a teammate's previous startup (DataMask).
4. **Most awards were sponsor prizes, and teams stacked them.** 22 of MHacks 2024's 30 awards were sponsor prizes; about 23% of the 132 submissions won something. EscapeMate used Groq, BoundaryML and Cartesia together. The WiLi Watch used FREE-WILi, Groq and Cartesia. Healthcare Helper used Breadboard and Cartesia. Winners of tool-specific prizes (Breadboard, BAML, Intel OpenVINO/Gaudi, Roboflow) used that sponsor's product in the core of the app, not as an add-on.
5. **Hardware wins its own lane.** EscapeMate (Raspberry Pi), The WiLi Watch (FREE-WILi, Arduino, Orange Pi), Wili-Party (FREE-WILi), Insight (Raspberry Pi wearable) and Cosmocook (HoloLens) all won awards. **FREE-WILi sponsored in 2024 and is back as a 2026 sponsor track**, and both of its 2024 hardware winners used the board.
6. **Developer tools are a reliable path to a prize.** Gemini Forge and Rusteze took 2nd and 3rd at Google x MHacks. At MHacks 2024, Ensemble, GenHint, Cyberwright, plzfix.ai and monotor won developer-tool or agent sponsor prizes.
7. **Solo and small teams can win.** Pixzip (Optimization), Msign (Education), Gemini Forge (Google 2nd), AstroChat VR and monotor were all one-person projects. The average MHacks 2024 winning team was about 3.2 people.
8. **Winners document their work.** Of the 30 MHacks 2024 winners, 22 have a video on Devpost and 25 link a GitHub repo. Their write-ups follow the template closely: inspiration with a statistic, architecture, challenges.
9. **Experienced hackers win repeatedly.** At least two people appear on winning teams at both 2024 events, according to the Devpost team lists (DataMask→AIDJ; Insight→WiLi Watch).

### Takeaways for MHacks 2026

- **Main track:** in 2024, most winners would map to **Actually Intelligent**, so expect that track to be the most crowded. Less competitive routes with a record of winning are **Beyond the Code (Hardware)**, **Sustainability** (FarmX, SolarVista and the load-balancing project all won), and **FinTech** (only one 2024 winner, predicTerminal, came close).
- **Build something you can demo in person:** XR, physical hardware, or live camera/vision. Every top-placed 2024 project could be shown live in a few seconds.
- **Name the user and give one statistic** in the first line of the pitch, and add a local U-M or Michigan hook if you can.
- **Target 2–3 sponsor prizes deliberately, and use each sponsor's tool at the core of the app.** For 2026, the **FREE-WILi** track has a 2024 record: both FREE-WILi hardware projects won awards. Voice output (Cartesia in 2024) maps to **ElevenLabs**, agent/developer-tool winners map to **Fetch.ai ASI:One / Relay**, and a polished UI like SignVerse's maps to **Figma best design**.
- **Use AI where it does real work**, such as a model you trained, a self-correcting loop, or function calling. The 2024 winners went deeper than a single chatbot call (FocusFlow's own model, Rusteze's compile-and-fix loop, Pixzip's GAN).
- **Fun tracks:** Boogie Battle (celebrity-voice dance commentary), osulation! (flailing-arms rhythm game) and AIDJ show that playful CV/voice projects can win sponsor prizes. Similar ideas fit **Useless AI / Dumbest Idea**.
- **Submit a full package:** a 1–3 minute video, a public GitHub repo, and a Devpost write-up following the standard headings. Most 2024 winners had all three.

### 6. Analysis: which judging signal is most actionable for 2026

1. **The 3-minute table-side pitch, heard by several judges, decides everything.** This is the strongest signal. The 2024 schedule had no visible finalist stage, so every judge saw the project at the table in about 3 minutes and then asked questions. The criteria were innovation, technical complexity, usability, and presentation quality or theme fit. *Inference:* a team should rehearse a demo-first pitch that addresses each criterion and the required track, and always keep someone at the table during judging, because the same project gets judged more than once.
2. **Sponsor prizes are judged in parallel against published rubrics.** That makes them the highest expected-value lever: 22 of 30 awards in 2024 were sponsor prizes. Those rubrics consistently rewarded a live demo that works (BoundaryML), the sponsor's technology being **central to the project** (Cartesia, Google), and wow or fun (Warp, Google). Some sponsors judged by the spirit of the category rather than use of their own tool (Warp, Magic Loops).
3. **Expect one prize per project.** *Inference from the 2024 data:* EscapeMate stacked three sponsor tools but won only its track prize, and no project won twice. Use extra sponsor tools to improve your odds, but don't plan on winning several prizes.
4. **The top prize went to technical work the team did themselves, not to API calls.** The Grand Prize (V²/R, with its own circuit solver and no LLM) and the Runner-Up (FocusFlow, with a self-trained model) both fit this. *Inference.*

### Takeaways for MHacks 2026
- **Write a 3-minute pitch and rehearse it with a timer:** problem and user, then the live demo, then what's technically hard, then impact. Prepare answers for "how did you build it, what broke, what's next". Those are the Q&A topics organizers told 2024 teams to expect.
- **Name the main track (Sustainability / Actually Intelligent / FinTech / Beyond the Code) in the first 10 seconds.** "Adherence to Theme" was a published 2024 criterion and track entry was mandatory, which matches 2026's required main track.
- **Get each 2026 sponsor's rubric on day one and design for it.** In 2024 every sponsor published its criteria in the Resources Guide. Mapping 2024 rubrics to 2026 prizes:
  - **ElevenLabs** ≈ Cartesia's rubric: make voice central.
  - **Fetch.ai ASI:One / Relay agents** ≈ BoundaryML's rubric: it must run live and be robust.
  - **Figma best design** ≈ the "Usability" and UX criteria.
  - **FREE-WILi**: back in 2026, and in 2024 both FREE-WILi builds won something.
- **Fun tracks (Useless AI / Dumbest Idea / Judged by an LLM):** there is no 2024 precedent. The nearest signal is Google's 15% "Fun & Wow" weight and wins by playful sponsor-track entries (Boogie Battle, osulation!, Wili-Party). For "Judged by an LLM", *inference:* the Devpost write-up is probably what gets judged, so follow the standard headings that 2024 winners used.
- **Have one technical core you can say "we built this ourselves" about:** a model, a solver, or hardware. Both top-two 2024 projects did.
- **Make sure the demo works end to end before polishing.** "Completeness: does it demo live" was a literal 2024 rubric line, and "the whole system works" is the most common winner boast.
- **Don't assume you can win several prizes;** pick a primary sponsor target. *Inference from 30 prizes going to 30 different projects.*
