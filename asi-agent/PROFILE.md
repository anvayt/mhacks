# Hidden Rent

Hidden Rent helps Ann Arbor renters discover the predicted heating and cooling cost hidden in a rental listing. Send an Ann Arbor address or a Zillow, Redfin or Apartments.com link. We return the Hidden Rent API's predicted letter grade, uncertainty range, annual energy cost, hidden rent per month, carbon estimate and questions to ask the landlord.

## Try it

- “What would heating and cooling cost at 1514 Morton Ave, Ann Arbor, MI?”
- Answer the numbered question using a number or words such as “gas” or “double pane.”
- Say “options” for improvements. Priced effects are labelled **projected if completed**; unsupported actions remain tips without fabricated savings.
- Say “try” followed by option numbers to select a hypothetical scenario, then “ff 30” to simulate keeping those actions. This stores no commitment, grade change or verified saving.
- Follow the returned share/compare links or continue the same session in iMessage. Public links depend on the team's current deployed website and onboarding URLs; local-only previews are labelled.

## What makes the answers trustworthy

All energy, cost, carbon, grade and improvement figures come from Hidden Rent's API. ASI:One classifies intent and selects permitted answers; it never writes the financial or energy reply. Forecasts and improvements are predictions, not measured savings. Predicted heating and cooling are not a full utility bill or an inspection. Metered buildings and unsupported retrofit paths may have no priced improvements.

## Scope and privacy

City of Ann Arbor residential addresses only. The API resolves listing links; this agent does not scrape listings. Conversation state is held in memory per sender and is lost on restart. “Reset” forgets this agent's conversation; API report links may still exist. Do not send private utility photos or phone numbers here; use the existing web/iMessage flow. No resident account is created by this agent.
