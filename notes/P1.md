## Implemented and checked

- `GET /hc/capabilities` advertises optional parameters, endpoints and path/fuel/type restrictions. `/api` negotiates support; discovery failure means no optional capability.
- Bill anomaly noise: opt in through `noise_basis=within_building`; API `/calibrate` does so only when advertised. Winter p90 is **25.1584%** from **514 month observations in 67 buildings**, the absolute residual against each building's other-year weather fit. All **1,985** saved gas-month predictions reproduce exactly. The former floor survives as `estimate_error_floor`, and old direct requests retain it. This is temporal variability, not removal of bias for an unmetered home or proof that an action caused savings.
- New gas/unmetered commitment scenarios: professional whole-home air sealing, supported single-family attic insulation, wall insulation, a gas-to-cold-climate heat pump and a thermostat setback. Effects compose in one run and return methods, sources and assumptions. New effects remain placeholders for metered/electric paths. Attic effects also remain placeholders for multifamily/mobile roofs; a top-floor answer does not establish attic support. Thermostat behavior alone earns no smart-device rebate or GRH equipment points.
- Gas heat-pump conversion uses delivered heat divided by a cited **COP 2.75 engineering assumption**, not resistance-heat pricing. It may increase dollar costs. Load reductions are subadditive; the user-approved exception preserves physical prices when an expensive fuel switch violates dollar sum-of-parts. No artificial savings cap is imposed.
- `GET /hc/lookalikes` and capability-gated `/map` clouds: actual rented ResStock MI simulation counts, sorted sample ≤400, full-pool quantiles and matching rule for each answer prefix. Missing service/data retain the honest empty cloud. These are simulated peers, not measured neighbors or calibrated uncertainty bands.
- `accuracy.signed_error_quantiles` reports signed empirical errors by path/fuel/season/month. **The API's current bill bands are unchanged**; adoption needs inversion/reversal because the error is `prediction / actual - 1`.
- `year_built` caller override; genuine `cooling_code=0` means zero cooling and heating identical to omitting the cooling answer; `months[].heating.usd_exact` and `cooling.usd_exact` add unrounded prices without changing old rounded fields.
